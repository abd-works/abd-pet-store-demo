Thin slicing - PawPlace incremental backlog - plain text

Product: PawPlace - online pet store with in-store adoption visits.

Slicing intent (by value, store-first): each increment must put a working
capability in the hands of a real store. Earlier increments leave out polish,
payment depth, accounts, pets, returns, and marketing so a store gets a usable
product on day one. Pet appointments are held back until the e-commerce spine
is real.

Spine vs optional: mandatory commerce spine is catalog -> store -> cart ->
pay -> fulfill. Pets, accounts, multi-vendor payments, returns, marketing,
reviews, and admin polish are real work but not required for the smallest
store-supporting slice; they ride later increments.

Increment 1: Walk-in driver - find the store, see what's in stock
  Outcome: A customer can find the nearest store, browse the product
    catalog, see real-time stock availability at that store, and walk in to
    ask for it. Store gets value on day one. No checkout, no payment, no
    accounts.
  Slicing notes: Categories only - no keyword search. Manual stock updates
    by staff via a bare-bones admin form. No cart, no notifications.
    Validates catalog data model, store + geo model, stock contract.
    Per the domain decision, walk-in point-of-sale (paying at the counter)
    is in scope for a later phase; today the in-store transaction itself is
    the customer talking to staff, not the system.
  Stories:
    - View Store Map
    - View Store List
    - Calculate Distance to Store
    - View Product Details
    - Display Real-Time Stock Availability
    - Update Product Stock Levels (admin, manual)

Increment 2: Click-and-collect - buy online, pick up at the store
  Outcome: Customer puts products in a cart, pays online with a card, and
    picks the order up at a chosen store. Store gets online revenue without
    anyone solving home-delivery logistics. Single payment vendor.
  Slicing notes: Guest checkout only. StripeWave only. Confirmation email
    from a static template. Pickup is the only fulfillment path. Manual
    pick-prep by store staff. Validates the riskiest payment integration on
    a real journey end-to-end.
  Stories:
    - Add Product to Cart
    - Update Cart Quantity
    - Remove Product from Cart
    - Select Click-and-Collect Store
    - Check Out as Guest
    - Enter Billing Address
    - Select Payment Method (StripeWave only)
    - Process Card Payment via StripeWave
    - Confirm Order and Send Confirmation Email
    - Prepare Click-and-Collect Orders for Pickup (admin)
    - Fulfill Click-and-Collect Order (admin)

Increment 3: Ship to home - full standard-delivery e-commerce
  Outcome: Same purchase journey but shipped to a home address. Store
    reaches customers outside its catchment.
  Slicing notes: Still guest checkout. Still StripeWave only. Standard
    delivery only - defer express and same-day. Manual shipping label
    creation. Validates order lifecycle past pickup: shipping notifications,
    tracking, order status.
  Stories:
    - Enter Shipping Address
    - Select Delivery Option (standard only)
    - View and Process Incoming Orders (admin)
    - Send Shipping Notification with Tracking Number
    - Track Order Status

Increment 4: Returning customers - accounts, history, reorder
  Outcome: Customers can register, log in, save addresses and payment
    methods, see order history, manage a wishlist, and one-click reorder.
    Account creation prompted during guest checkout. Lifts repeat-purchase
    rate without changing the buy flow.
  Slicing notes: Email + password only - no social login. Email verification
    mandatory. Reliable session management across devices is non-negotiable.
    Saved address and saved payment method land here as concepts (per the
    domain decision); checkout gains the option to pick from saved entities
    for logged-in customers.
  Stories:
    - Register Account
    - Send Email Verification
    - Verify Email Address
    - Log In
    - Log Out
    - Reset Password
    - Maintain Session Across Devices
    - Save Delivery Address
    - Manage Saved Addresses
    - Save Payment Method
    - Manage Saved Payment Methods
    - Select Saved Address at Checkout
    - Select Saved Payment Method at Checkout
    - View Order History
    - Manage Wishlist
    - Reorder Previous Purchase

Increment 5: Pay your way - multi-vendor payment with retries
  Outcome: Customers pay with PayNova (mobile wallet) and VaultPay (BNPL)
    in addition to StripeWave. Failed payments retry automatically across
    all three. Lifts conversion among younger buyers and basket size on
    premium items.
  Slicing notes: Proves the payment vendor abstraction generalises beyond
    StripeWave. Webhook handling and failed-payment retries land together.
    Refund routing is in scope so Increment 7 builds cleanly.
  Stories:
    - Process Digital Wallet Payment via PayNova
    - Process Buy-Now-Pay-Later via VaultPay
    - Retry Failed Payment

Increment 6: Pet visits - gallery and in-store appointments
  Outcome: Adoption side goes live. Customers browse the pet gallery, see
    which store a pet is at and how far away, and book an appointment to
    visit. Store staff see incoming bookings. Pet status admin-managed.
  Slicing notes: Held back until now per the explicit guidance that pet
    appointments can be later. Confirmation and day-before reminder ride on
    the transactional notification rails. Single-store flow first if
    multi-store scheduling is too complex. Per the domain decision,
    appointment booking is customer-account-only; an account-gate is part
    of Confirm Appointment Booking AC. The pet-adopted-before-visit
    notification + the customer-side Cancel or Rebook flow ride here too
    because they are inseparable from the appointment lifecycle.
  Stories:
    - Browse Pets by Species
    - View Pet Profile
    - View Pet Store Location and Distance
    - View Available Time Slots at Store
    - Select Date and Time Slot
    - Add Visit Note
    - Confirm Appointment Booking (account-gated)
    - View Upcoming and Past Appointments (customer)
    - Cancel or Rebook Appointment After Pet Adoption (customer)
    - Update Pet Profile (admin)
    - Mark Pet as Adopted (admin)
    - View Incoming Appointments (admin)
    - Send Appointment Reminder (transactional)
    - Send Pet Adopted Before Visit Notification (transactional)

Increment 7: Returns and refunds - close the loop
  Outcome: Customers can initiate a return from order history, get a
    printable label or QR code, and watch the refund land back on the
    original payment method. In-store returns are reflected in the
    customer's account too.
  Slicing notes: The vendor-routing invariant on refund drives this slice -
    refund must always route through the vendor that took the original
    payment. Online return flow first; in-store reconciliation second.
  Stories:
    - Initiate Return from Order History
    - Generate Return Label or QR Code
    - Route Refund through Original Payment Vendor
    - Track Refund Status
    - Process In-Store Return (admin)
    - Send Return and Refund Status Update (transactional)

Increment 8: Marketing engine - reviews, alerts, and content
  Outcome: Drives repeat traffic and conversion through customer reviews
    (social proof), opt-in marketing emails, restock alerts, personalised
    recommendations, and a content stream of blog posts and pet care guides.
    Engagement features only - the buy flow is unchanged.
  Slicing notes: Marketing must respect the explicit-opt-in invariant on
    communication preferences - no broadcast without opt-in. Transactional
    notifications stay independent of marketing rails. Content publishing
    is admin-only at first.
  Stories:
    - Submit Written Review with Star Rating
    - Submit Photo Review
    - Read Customer Reviews
    - Set Notification Preferences
    - Set Communication Preferences
    - Opt In to Marketing Email List
    - Send Promotional Email
    - Send Personalized Recommendation
    - Send Restock Alert
    - Send In-Store Event Notification
    - Unsubscribe from Marketing Emails
    - Send Order Confirmation (if not delivered earlier in Increment 2)
    - Send Shipping Update with Tracking (if not delivered earlier in Inc 3)
    - Publish Blog Post
    - Publish Pet Care Guide

Increment 9: Power-ups - search, personalization, admin polish
  Outcome: Smart search lifts conversion on a deep catalog; store
    personalization and customer pet profiles tighten loyalty; the admin
    inventory dashboard replaces the bare-bones stock form from Increment 1.
    Polish over a fully-functional product.
  Slicing notes: Each story adds value but none is required for the core
    commerce or adoption journeys. Sequence within follows whichever
    stakeholder pulls hardest.
  Stories:
    - Search Products by Keyword
    - Filter Products
    - Filter Stores by Availability and Specialization
    - Set My Store Preference
    - Tailor Experience to Preferred Store
    - Create Pet Profile (customer's own pets)
    - Update Pet Profile (customer's own)
    - View Inventory Dashboard (admin)
